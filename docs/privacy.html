Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

Bargaz is a personal software project run by Devon Finney-Gaitan. This policy covers bargaz.app, bot.bargaz.app, live.bargaz.app, and randomwolf08.com.

RandomWolf08 may later become a separate site with its own documents. Until that happens, this policy covers it.

2. The short version

  • There are no accounts, logins, or user profiles.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • The only thing stored about you on our server is an appearance setting tied to a random identifier your browser creates.
  • Advertising is provided by Google and uses Google's cookies.
  • Server logs are kept for 30 days.

3. What we collect

Appearance settings. Whether you chose light or dark theme, high contrast, or colorblind mode. Your browser creates a random identifier, and your settings are stored on our server under that identifier so they follow you between our sites. The identifier contains no name, email address, or account information.

Server logs. IP address, browser user agent, requested page, time, and response status. Cloudflare and our server record these to keep the sites running, detect abuse, and troubleshoot faults.

Messages you send us. If you email us or contact us on Discord, we receive whatever you include.

Aggregate analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics reports page views, referrers, country, and device type. It does not use cookies and does not track individuals across sites.

What we do not collect. No account or password, no payment details, no precise location, no contact lists, and no advertising profiles of our own.

4. Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies ourselves. To remember your settings, your browser stores a few values in local storage: your random identifier, your theme and accessibility choices, and a short-lived key used to carry those choices between our domains. Clearing site data in your browser removes them.

Cloudflare may set security cookies, such as __cf_bm, to tell human visitors apart from automated traffic.

5. Advertising

When advertising is enabled, ads are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar identifiers to show ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this and other sites. We do not control those cookies. We do not receive your name or contact details from Google; we receive aggregate reports and estimated earnings.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we ask for consent through Google's certified consent tool before using advertising cookies. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time from the privacy settings link shown with the ads.

You can limit personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings, aboutads.info, or youronlinechoices.eu. Opting out of personalized ads does not remove ads.

6. How we use information

We use it to run and secure the sites, remember your appearance settings, answer your messages, understand aggregate traffic, serve advertising, and meet legal obligations. We do not build profiles of individuals and we do not make automated decisions that affect your rights.

7. Who we share it with

  • Cloudflare, for DNS, content delivery, security, and analytics.
  • Google, for advertising and advertising consent.
  • GitHub, to read the public content shown on some pages.
  • Our email provider, to receive and reply to messages.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or if disclosure is necessary to protect rights or safety.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with data brokers.

8. Where information is processed

The sites are hosted on a privately operated server in the United States. Cloudflare and Google process information worldwide. Where the law requires safeguards for international transfers, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

9. How long we keep it

  • Appearance settings: until you clear your browser data or the record is removed. The store holds at most 10,000 records and discards the oldest first.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Analytics: aggregate only, retained according to Cloudflare's terms.
  • Messages: for as long as needed to handle them, plus a reasonable record afterwards.
  • Advertising data: controlled by Google.

We do not keep information we no longer need.

10. Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. The server is privately operated with restricted access, and we store as little as possible. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

11. Your choices and rights

You can clear your settings at any time by clearing site data in your browser, and you can change or withdraw advertising consent from the privacy settings link shown with the ads.

You can also ask what is stored under your identifier, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing devon6608@gmail.com. We may need to confirm the identifier belongs to you. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

12. Children

The sites are not directed at children under 13 in the United States or under 16 in the EEA and UK. Please do not send us personal information if you are under that age. If we learn that we hold such information, we will delete it.

13. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The sites do not track you across other websites. Advertising partners handle browser privacy signals according to their own policies.

14. Other sites and BargazBot

Links to Discord, GitHub, YouTube, and Linktree lead to services with their own privacy policies. BargazBot is a separate product: how it handles Discord data is described in BargazBot's own privacy policy, not this one.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top always shows the current version, and significant changes will be noted on the site.
